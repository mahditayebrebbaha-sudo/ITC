Cosmic Cookies 🍪

The easiest chocolate-chip cookies in this galaxy.

⏰ 25 minutes | 🍪 12 cookies | ⭐ Super easy

What you'll need

  • 1 cup of flour
  • 1/2 cup of butter
  • Sugar
  • Egg
  • Chocolate chips

Let's bake!

  1. Preheat the oven to 175 °C and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Cream the butter and sugar, then mix in flour, baking soda, and chocolate chips.
  3. Scoop dough onto the sheet and bake for 10–12 minutes until edges are golden.
  4. Let the cookies cool on the sheet for 5 minutes before transferring.

💡Chilling the dough for 30 minutes gives thicker, chewier cookies.
